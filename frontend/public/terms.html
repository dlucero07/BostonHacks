On-the-Go prototype terms

On-the-Go is a hackathon prototype for discovering food stops on Boston journeys. All demo places, ratings, prices, opening status, route lines and travel times are simulated. Demo map coordinates do not represent real restaurant locations.

Live data is supplied by Google. By using live Google features, you are also bound by Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service. Review Google’s privacy policy.

Travel times are estimates and exclude eating, waiting, parking and service. Walking routes can be missing sidewalks or pedestrian paths; use caution and follow local conditions. Opening times, prices and dietary attributes may change. Confirm dietary needs directly with the restaurant. This app does not guarantee restaurant suitability or availability.
