On-the-Go privacy

This hackathon prototype does not create user accounts. In live mode, the locations you enter are sent to Google to find places and routes. Precise device location is requested only when you choose “Use my location.” Origin and destination are held in temporary server memory for up to 15 minutes and are not saved to the application database.

We record restaurant place IDs, action types (selection, details, add stop), server timestamps and an anonymous random session identifier. We do not include your name, email or route in those events. The session identifier is stored in browser session storage. Events measure unique recent interest. Demo storage is temporary unless a database is configured.

In live mode, Google Maps and Places are subject to Google’s privacy policy. Your Google Maps navigation handoff opens Google's service. Hosting providers may process operational connection data.
